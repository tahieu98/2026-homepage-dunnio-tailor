How do I choose between a suit and a tuxedo?+
Start with your occasion and its dress code. Explore the suit and tuxedo guide, or ask the team for advice.
What if I’m unsure about measurements?+
Read how ordering works before submitting your measurements. If anything is unclear, contact Dunnio for guidance.
How much will my garment cost?+
Categories display starting prices in USD. Your fabric and design choices affect the final price. Review the order total and applicable delivery charges before committing.
Can I order for a specific event date?+
Ask Dunnio to confirm the production and delivery schedule for your location and garment. These concept pages do not promise a delivery date.
What happens if the fit needs attention?+
Read the current Fit Guarantee and Shipping & Returns terms, then contact the service team with your concerns.
